# Hacktoberfest 2026 Announcement Draft

Hacktoberfest 2026 is officially here, and this year, AI belongs to everyone! Hosted throughout October by Major League Hacking and DEV in partnership with DigitalOcean, this month-long celebration brings developers, students, and open-source creators together across the globe.

Get ready for an exciting hybrid experience featuring more than 300 in-person community Fests alongside global online activities, including hands-on DEV Challenges, interactive MLH livestreams, and Global Hack Week sessions. Forget repetitive pull requests—this year's refreshed format celebrates genuine learning and creation with open-source AI and open-weight models. Complete your participant dashboard activities to earn an exclusive sticker pack delivered right to your doorstep, plus collectible DEV profile badges and limited-edition swag at local Fests.

Whether you are stepping into artificial intelligence for the very first time or shipping cutting-edge open models, your community is waiting. Sign in to My Hacktoberfest with your MyMLH account, register for a nearby Fest or join DEV Challenges online, and start building with open-source AI today!
